Rm8tes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Rm8tes is an app that helps renters in the UK protect their tenancy deposit: it captures move-in and move-out photo evidence, keeps a tenancy diary, and generates an evidence pack you can share with your landlord or a deposit scheme.

This policy explains what personal information Rm8tes collects, why, where it is stored, and the choices and rights you have. We have tried to write it in plain English.

Who is responsible for your data (the "data controller"): Roomates Ltd, trading as Rm8tes — a company registered in England and Wales, company number 16826260, registered office 35 Welbeck Avenue, Tunbridge Wells, England, TN4 9BD. Contact: rm8tes@gmail.com

In this policy, "Rm8tes", "we", and "us" all mean Roomates Ltd.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your account details, your profile, and the tenancy records and photos you choose to capture.
  • Your data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in London, United Kingdom.
  • We do not sell your data or show ads, and the app contains no advertising or marketing technology.
  • We use two privacy-focused tools, both hosted in the EU: PostHog to see which features are used, and Sentry to receive crash reports. Neither ever receives your photos, diary entries, addresses, or anything else you capture as evidence.
  • Location is optional, used only at the moment you take an evidence photo, and only to stamp that photo.
  • Evidence packs (PDFs) are generated on your device and are only shared if and where you choose to share them.
  • You can delete your account in the app, and you can ask us to erase everything we hold about you.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and stored only in hashed form — we never see or store your plain-text password. Your email address is used to sign you in, to send account emails (such as email confirmation and password-reset links), and is stored alongside your profile.

Profile information

You can choose to add profile details: display name, age, whether you are a student or intern, gender, profession, general location (as free text, e.g. a city), monthly room budget, a short bio, lifestyle tags, and a profile photo. Apart from a display name, all of these are optional. Gender includes a "prefer not to say" option.

Profile photo: if you add one, it is stored in a publicly accessible storage bucket, which means anyone who has the photo's web address can view it. Do not upload a profile photo you would not want to be publicly visible.

Property and tenancy information

When you log a property or tenancy, we store what you enter: the property address, your move-in date, your deposit amount, and your landlord's name and (optionally) email address. If you use the viewing checklist, we also store the addresses of properties you view and your checklist notes.

Evidence and records you capture

  • Audit photos: move-in and move-out photos are taken with the camera (never selected from your photo library), and each is stored with the date and time it was taken and — if you allow location access — the GPS coordinates where it was taken. These photos are stored in a private storage bucket that only your account can access.
  • Damage checklist: the condition items you tick off and their severity.
  • Living diary: the dated entries you write (title, description, date).

Evidence packs (PDFs)

When you generate a deposit evidence pack, the PDF is built on your device. It contains your display name, the property address, landlord details, deposit amount, and your audit photos with their timestamps and location stamps. We do not upload or store the PDF. If you share it (for example by AirDrop, email, or a messaging app), it goes only to the destination you choose, through your device's share sheet.

Device permissions

  • Camera — to take your profile photo and to capture audit evidence. Optional; the app asks before first use.
  • Photo library — only so you can choose an existing photo as your profile photo. We do not scan or upload your library.
  • Location (while using the app) — only at the moment you take an audit photo, to stamp it with GPS coordinates so it is stronger tenancy evidence. This is optional and best-effort: if you decline, or no fix is available, the photo is simply saved without coordinates. The app does not track your location in the background or at any other time.

Stored on your device only

Your light/dark theme preference and your sign-in session token are stored locally on your device. They are not used for tracking.

Usage analytics and crash reports

To understand which features are useful and to fix problems, we use two service providers:

  • PostHog (usage analytics). The app records when you complete key actions — creating an account, finishing your profile, adding a property, taking move-in audit photos, using the viewing checklist, adding a diary entry, logging damage, generating an evidence pack, and opening the Know Your Rights section — along with basic technical information such as your device model, operating system version, and app version. Events are linked to a random account identifier, never to your email address. The content of what you enter or capture — photos, property addresses, diary text, checklist notes, landlord details — is never sent to PostHog. We do not use session recording.
  • Sentry (crash reporting). If the app crashes or encounters an error, a report is sent containing the technical details of the error (such as the stack trace) and the same basic device information, so we can find and fix the problem.

Both services act as our processors under data processing agreements, are configured to store data in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), and do not use your data for their own purposes or for advertising.

What we do not collect

We do not show ads and use no advertising or marketing SDKs. We do not collect your contacts, your browsing history, or your precise location outside of photo capture. We do not use your data to train AI models. The analytics and crash reports described above never include the content of your evidence or records.

Information about other people

To describe your tenancy, you may enter another person's details — typically your landlord's name and email address. This information is stored only as part of your tenancy records and appears in evidence packs you generate; we do not use it for any other purpose and never contact your landlord ourselves. Please only enter information about others that is accurate and relevant to your tenancy.

How we use your information, and our legal bases

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), we need a legal basis for using your data:

  • To provide the service (performance of a contract): creating and operating your account, storing your profile, tenancy records, photos, checklists, and diary, and generating evidence packs.
  • With your consent: attaching GPS coordinates to audit photos (you can decline the location permission at any time in your device settings; existing photos keep the stamp they were saved with).
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure (for example, authentication and access controls); communicating essential account information such as password-reset emails; and understanding, through the limited usage analytics and crash reports described above, how the app's features are used and where it fails, so we can improve it. We have designed this to be minimal: feature-level events and technical crash data only, under a random identifier, never the content of your records.

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making.

Where your data is stored and who can access it

Your account, profile, and records are stored using Supabase, our backend and hosting provider, in their London (United Kingdom) region. Supabase processes data on our behalf under its data processing agreement and does not use your data for its own purposes. Access to your records is restricted so that only your signed-in account can read or change them (with the exception of profile photos, which are stored at a publicly accessible address as described above).

Account emails, such as email confirmation and password-reset messages, are sent through Supabase's authentication service.

Usage analytics are processed for us by PostHog and crash reports by Sentry, both in their European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) regions and only on our instructions, as described in "Usage analytics and crash reports" above.

We do not share your personal information with anyone else. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with advertisers or data brokers. We would disclose information only if genuinely required by law, or as part of a transfer of the service itself (in which case this policy would continue to apply and you would be notified).

Anything you export yourself — such as sharing an evidence pack PDF — is under your control and goes only to the recipients you choose.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists, so that your evidence remains available for the length of your tenancy and any deposit dispute. When your account is deleted, or you ask us to erase your data, we delete your records from our live systems without undue delay.

Copies of your data may survive for a short while in backups we keep so that the service can be recovered after a failure. We do not use those backups for anything else, and we cannot pick a single person's data out of one — instead, your data disappears from them as older backups are replaced by newer ones, which takes no more than 30 days. In the unlikely event that we ever had to restore the service from a backup taken before your deletion, we would re-apply your deletion straight afterwards.

Crash reports are deleted by Sentry automatically after 90 days. Usage analytics events are kept under their random identifier so we can see longer-term trends; when we erase your data on request, this includes the analytics events linked to your account's identifier.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy;
  • Rectify inaccurate data (most profile and tenancy details can be edited directly in the app);
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Restrict or object to our processing of your data;
  • Data portability — receive data you provided in a machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it (for example, by turning off the location permission).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at rm8tes@gmail.com. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, though we would appreciate the chance to help first.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account from the profile screen in the app. This erases everything we hold about you — your login credentials, your profile and photo, every property and tenancy record, every evidence photo, your damage checklists, your diary entries, and your usage analytics. It happens immediately and it cannot be undone; we have no way to recover a deleted account for you afterwards.

Because deletion is permanent, generate and save any PDF evidence pack you might still need — for a deposit dispute, for example — before you delete. Anything you exported beforehand is outside our systems and is unaffected.

If you would rather we handled the erasure, or you can no longer sign in to your account, email us at rm8tes@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will complete it and confirm within one month (usually much sooner).

Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Access to your records is enforced at the database level (row-level security), so your evidence, diary, and tenancy details are only readable by your own signed-in account. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but if we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data we will notify you and the ICO as required by law.

Children

Rm8tes is intended for renters aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other services

The app's "Know Your Rights" section links to external websites such as GOV.UK, Shelter, and Citizens Advice. Opening a link takes you out of the app; those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy — for example because the app gains new features — we will update this page and change the date at the top. For significant changes we will let you know in the app before they take effect.

Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints about privacy: rm8tes@gmail.com
