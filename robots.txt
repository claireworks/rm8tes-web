# robots.txt is a request, not a fence. Crawlers that respect it will read this
# and stay out; a scraper written to ignore it will not even fetch this file.
# What it does buy us is the on-the-record part: an operator who takes our
# content after this was published cannot claim they had no notice.
#
# The line this file draws is between two different things:
#
#   * Crawlers that COLLECT the site -- hoovering pages up for training corpora
#     or for a competitor's backlink tooling. They take and give nothing back.
#     Those are blocked below.
#
#   * Assistants that READ the site to answer somebody's question and cite us --
#     ChatGPT search, Perplexity and friends. Those are deliberately still
#     allowed. Blocking them would mean we stop appearing as an answer when
#     someone asks an assistant about deposit-evidence apps, and for a product
#     nobody has heard of yet that referral is worth more than the copying risk
#     it avoids. See the commented-out group at the bottom -- that is the
#     decision, not an oversight, and reversing it is one uncomment.
#
# Note that Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended are training-only opt-outs:
# neither affects Google Search ranking or Apple's Spotlight/Siri results, so
# blocking them below costs us no discoverability at all.
